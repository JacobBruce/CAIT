# Genre Quick-Starts

Suggested defaults for a fast setup. Offer one as a base, then let the player tweak any
field. All values are starting points, not constraints — write the final choices into
`WORLD.md`.

Each preset suggests: tone, content rating, mechanics tier, resolution mode, death
model, a sample premise, a starting location, and an art style.

## High Fantasy
- **Tone:** heroic, adventurous, a touch of wonder.
- **Content rating:** moderate (stylized violence, light romance).
- **Mechanics:** light stats. **Resolution:** GM-adjudicated.
- **Death:** downed-not-dead in most fights; death reserved for dramatic stakes.
- **Premise:** a frontier realm where old ruins stir and a forgotten threat wakes.
- **Start:** a village tavern at the edge of the wilds.
- **Art style:** painterly fantasy illustration.

## Grimdark Survival
- **Tone:** bleak, tense, morally grey.
- **Content rating:** mature (graphic violence, harsh consequences) — confirm limits.
- **Mechanics:** full stats + checks. **Resolution:** real rolls via `repl_exec`.
- **Death:** permadeath; injuries linger and resources are scarce.
- **Premise:** a dying land where every choice costs something and trust is currency.
- **Start:** a ruined waystation in hostile territory.
- **Art style:** desaturated grimdark concept art.

## Hopeful Sci-Fi
- **Tone:** exploratory, optimistic, wonder-driven.
- **Content rating:** moderate.
- **Mechanics:** light stats. **Resolution:** GM-adjudicated.
- **Death:** narrative consequence; setbacks over outright death.
- **Premise:** first survey of an uncharted system aboard a small crew ship.
- **Start:** the bridge of the survey vessel as it drops out of FTL.
- **Art style:** clean, luminous sci-fi concept art.

## Modern Mystery
- **Tone:** grounded, suspenseful, investigative.
- **Content rating:** moderate (crime, tension).
- **Mechanics:** freeform, with a few tracked clues/leads.
- **Resolution:** GM-adjudicated.
- **Death:** rare; stakes are reputation, secrets, and danger closing in.
- **Premise:** a quiet town where a disappearance unravels into something larger.
- **Start:** the protagonist's apartment, the morning the case lands.
- **Art style:** moody cinematic noir.

## Post-Apocalyptic
- **Tone:** weary, scrappy, glimmers of hope.
- **Content rating:** mature (violence, scarcity) — confirm limits.
- **Mechanics:** full stats + checks. **Resolution:** real rolls via `repl_exec`.
- **Death:** permadeath; hunger, injury, and supplies matter.
- **Premise:** decades after the collapse, a rumor of a working settlement spreads.
- **Start:** a scavenger's roadside camp at dusk.
- **Art style:** weathered post-apocalyptic realism.
